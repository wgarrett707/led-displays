Drops: one white flash (or a white hit then a strobe for as many bars as you set, in the drop panel; never over 3 flashes a second). Lifts: one big wave of colour.
Fade out: the strips fade to black over its length and stay dark. Balls: balls bounce and collide, more on every beat, until they fill the strips.
Quiet: pick "quiet" as a build-up speed (or Add a quiet stretch) where the music cuts out before the drop; the strips go dark until it hits.
Build-ups: pulses rush in from the far ends at the speeds in the Build-up panel; the cyan ticks under the waveform are where each pulse fires.
Click track: high click on bar 1, low clicks on the other beats.
